# Shipping and delivery information

Standard orders ship within two business days. Cold-chain orders ship Monday to Wednesday only so they do not sit in transit over a weekend.

| Option | Typical delivery | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard ground | 3 to 5 business days | $6.50, free over $60 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.00 |
| Cold-chain express | 1 to 2 business days | $18.00 |
| Local delivery (Marlow Falls area) | Same or next day | $5.00 |

Local deliveries are made by an independent courier. You receive a text message when your package is on the way.

If your order is late, email care@juniperrow.example with your order number and we will check on it the same day.
