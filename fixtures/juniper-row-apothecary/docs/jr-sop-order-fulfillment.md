# SOP: Order fulfillment

Owner: store manager. Applies to all fulfillment associates.

## Daily flow

1. Open the order queue at 9:30 am and again at 1:00 pm.
2. Print pick lists in order of promised ship date, oldest first.
3. Pick each item and check the label against the pick list.
4. Check batch codes and expiry dates. Pull anything with fewer than 90 days left and tell the manager.
5. Pack with the correct box size. Add the packing slip and a care card.
6. Weigh, label and scan the package. Move it to the outbound shelf for its carrier.
7. Mark the order shipped in the queue so the customer gets a tracking email.

## Exceptions

- Out of stock item: hold the order and tell customer care. Do not substitute without approval.
- Damaged item: set it aside, take a photo and note the batch code.
- Address problem: send to customer care, who contacts the customer.

Specialty orders are packed only by the manager or a trained associate.
