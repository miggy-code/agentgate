# SOP: Cold-chain shipping

Cold-chain items (marked in the catalog) must stay cool from our fridge to the customer's door.

## Packing

- Pre-chill insulated liners in the walk-in fridge for at least 4 hours.
- Use two cold packs for ground shipments and three for summer months (June to September).
- Place the item in the center, with a layer of paper filler on top.
- Tape the box on all seams and apply the "Keep cool" label.

## Shipping days

Ship Monday to Wednesday only. If an order is ready on Thursday, hold it in the fridge until Monday and tell the customer.

## Temperature log

Record the fridge reading at open and close each day on the log sheet. If the reading is outside the range printed on the sheet, tell the manager before packing anything.

## Problems in transit

If a customer reports a warm package, customer care opens a replacement order and the manager decides whether to file a carrier claim.
