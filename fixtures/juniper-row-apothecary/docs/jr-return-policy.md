# Return policy

We want you to be happy with your order. This policy applies to orders placed on juniperrow.example and at the shop.

## What can be returned

- Unopened, sealed products within 30 days of delivery.
- Items that arrive damaged or leaking. Please send a photo within 5 days of delivery.
- Items we shipped by mistake.

## What cannot be returned

- Opened or used skin-care products, except for damage on arrival.
- Specialty orders made to a customer's request, unless we made an error.
- Cold-chain items that were left out of refrigeration after delivery.

## How to start a return

Email care@juniperrow.example with your order number. We reply with a return label or a pickup time. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of receiving the item.
