# Commercial bid in progress: Harlan Creek Elementary rooftop units

**Status:** Draft. Bid is due on the first Friday of next month at the district office. Do not share outside the company.

**Scope:** Replace six aging rooftop packaged units with new 5 ton units, new curbs, controls integration, crane day and commissioning. Work to be done over the summer break.

| Line | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Equipment (6 units) | $54,000 |
| Curb adapters and roof work | $8,400 |
| Crane and rigging | $6,200 |
| Controls and thermostats | $7,100 |
| Labor (approx. 340 hours) | $27,500 |
| Permits and inspection | $1,800 |
| Contingency (6%) | $6,300 |
| **Total cost** | **$111,300** |
| **Proposed bid price** | **$146,500** |

**Strategy notes:** We think two competitors will bid between $139,000 and $155,000. Our margin at the proposed price is about 24%, below our normal floor, accepted because of the multi-year maintenance contract the district has hinted at. Owner approval is required to go lower than $142,000.

**Open items:** Confirm crane availability, ask the supplier about unit lead times, confirm prevailing wage requirements.
