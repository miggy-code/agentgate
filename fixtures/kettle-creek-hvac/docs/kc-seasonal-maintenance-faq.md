# Seasonal maintenance FAQ

**How often should I service my system?** Once a year for each system: heating in early fall, cooling in spring. A combo visit does both.

**How often do I change the filter?** Every 1 to 3 months. Pleated filters in a house with pets need changing closer to every month.

**What happens during a tune-up?** We check airflow, clean the burners or coil as needed, test safety controls, measure temperature split, inspect the drain line and look at electrical connections.

**My furnace smells odd the first time each fall.** A light dusty smell for a few minutes is normal. A gas smell is not. Leave the house and call your gas utility first, then us.

**My air conditioner is freezing up.** Turn it off, switch the fan to on to melt the ice, and call us. Running it frozen can damage the compressor.

**Does a tune-up lower my bill?** Often a little, mostly because a clean system moves air better. The bigger benefit is catching small failures before they become winter emergencies.
