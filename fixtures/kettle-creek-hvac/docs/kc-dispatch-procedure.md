# Dispatch procedure

The dispatcher owns the schedule from 7:00 a.m. until the last truck is back.

**Taking a call**
1. Get name, address, phone and a one-line description of the problem.
2. Ask: is anyone without heat in freezing weather, is there a gas smell, is there water damage. Any yes goes to emergency priority.
3. Create the job ticket and give the customer an arrival window of two hours.

**Priority order**
1. Gas smell, carbon monoxide alarm, no heat below freezing for elderly or infants.
2. No heat or no cooling.
3. Partial failure or noise.
4. Scheduled maintenance.

**Assigning**
- Match the technician to the job: installs go to the lead tech, maintenance to either field tech.
- Overflow and weekend overflow go to the contractor, with the service manager's approval.
- Send the ticket to the assigned technician before 7:30 a.m. the day of the job.

**Closing out**
The technician updates the ticket with work done, parts used and refrigerant weights. The office manager invoices within one business day.
