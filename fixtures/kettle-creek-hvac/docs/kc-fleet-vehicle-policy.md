# Fleet vehicle policy

Kettle Creek runs five service vans and one pickup. These rules apply to every employee who drives a company vehicle.

- Vehicles are for company business. Personal errands need the service manager's approval.
- Seat belts are required. No phone use while driving, hands-free or not, during a call.
- Complete the pre-trip walk-around each morning: tires, lights, ladder tie-downs, cylinder straps.
- Refrigerant cylinders ride upright and strapped, never in the cab.
- Report any damage or warning light the same day. The service manager schedules repairs.
- Fuel cards are for fuel and car washes only. Keep receipts.
- Parking at home is allowed only for the technician assigned to the vehicle. Keep it locked and keep tools inside.
- Contractors drive their own vehicles and carry their own insurance.

Violations are handled by the service manager and may lead to loss of vehicle privileges.
