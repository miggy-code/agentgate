# Warranty terms

**Labor.** Repairs and installations by Kettle Creek carry a 12 month labor warranty from the completion date. If the same fault returns inside that window we come back at no labor charge.

**Parts.** Parts installed by us carry the manufacturer's parts warranty, usually 1 to 10 years depending on the part. We handle the claim paperwork for you.

**Equipment registration.** Many manufacturers extend their warranty only if the new equipment is registered within 60 days. We register every system we install and give you a copy of the confirmation.

**What is not covered.**
- Damage from power surges, flooding or fire.
- Problems caused by dirty or missing air filters.
- Work done by another contractor after our visit.
- Refrigerant lost through damage that is not our workmanship.

**Making a claim.** Call 555-0110 with your invoice number. We will schedule a visit within two business days for non-urgent claims.
