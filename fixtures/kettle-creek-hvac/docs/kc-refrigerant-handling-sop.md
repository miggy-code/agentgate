# SOP: Refrigerant handling

Only technicians with a current EPA Section 608 certification may handle refrigerant. The service manager keeps a copy of each certificate on file.

**Rules**
- Never vent refrigerant. Recover into an approved cylinder every time a system is opened.
- Weigh recovered refrigerant and write the amount on the job ticket.
- Do not mix refrigerant types in a cylinder. Label cylinders by type.
- Track every cylinder: serial number, tare weight, current weight, truck assignment.
- Leak repair: for systems over the threshold charge, repair a leak above the allowed rate within 30 days and record the date.

**Records**
Keep the service log for each system with the date, technician, refrigerant type, amount added and amount recovered. The office keeps these records for at least three years.

**Disposal**
Used cylinders and recovered refrigerant go to the reclaim supplier on the first Friday of the month. Ask the service manager for the pickup slip.

**Contractors**
Subcontracted technicians must show their certification before working on a sealed system and must follow this SOP on our jobs.
