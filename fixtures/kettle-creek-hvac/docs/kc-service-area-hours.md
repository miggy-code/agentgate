# Service area and business hours

**Office hours:** Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central.

**Service calls:** Monday to Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Emergency heating and cooling calls are answered 24 hours a day, with an after-hours surcharge.

**Area:** We serve the towns around Kettle Creek, including Dunmore Falls, Alder Ridge, Pinecrest and the surrounding townships, within about 30 miles of our shop.

**Contact:** 555-0110, service@kettlecreek.example, https://www.kettlecreek.example
