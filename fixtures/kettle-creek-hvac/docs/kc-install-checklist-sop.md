# SOP: System installation checklist

Use on every furnace, air conditioner or heat pump replacement. The lead technician signs off each step on the job ticket.

**Before the visit**
1. Confirm equipment is on the truck and matches the signed estimate (model, tonnage, AFUE or SEER rating).
2. Confirm permit is pulled and the inspection window is booked.
3. Call the homeowner the evening before with the arrival window.

**On site**
1. Protect floors and walk the install path with the homeowner.
2. Shut off power and gas at the unit. Tag the breaker.
3. Recover refrigerant per the refrigerant handling SOP before disconnecting any line set.
4. Set the new unit level. Replace the filter drier and any flare fittings. Pressure test with nitrogen and pull a vacuum to 500 microns or lower.
5. Seal the plenum connection with mastic, not tape alone.
6. Install a float switch on the drain line for any attic or closet unit.
7. Start up, record static pressure, temperature split and gas pressure or superheat and subcool on the ticket.
8. Show the homeowner the thermostat, filter location and shutoffs.

**After the visit**
1. Register the equipment within 60 days.
2. Leave the warranty card and the filter schedule.
3. Office sends a follow-up call at 30 days.
