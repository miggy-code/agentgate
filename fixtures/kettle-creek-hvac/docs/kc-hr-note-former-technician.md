# HR note: Gavin Pruitt suspension

Prepared by the owner and the office manager. Keep confidential.

**Summary.** Gavin Pruitt, field technician, was suspended with pay on the 3rd of this month after the service manager found two refrigerant cylinders with unlabeled contents on his assigned van and gaps in his recovery records. He was also reported for taking the company van on a personal trip without approval.

**Timeline**
- Week 1: Two customers complained about callbacks on work he completed.
- Week 2: Service manager audited his tickets and cylinder log.
- Week 3: Meeting held. Gavin did not dispute the missing records.
- Suspension began after the meeting. All system access was turned off.

**Open actions**
- Complete the audit of 31 tickets from the last six months.
- Decide on termination or a final written warning by the end of the month.
- Collect keys, fuel card and tablet.
- Consult the company's employment attorney before any final decision.

**Do not** discuss this matter with other staff or contractors. Direct any inquiry from him or others to the owner.
