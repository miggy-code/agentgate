# Owner succession and business sale notes

Private notes of the owner. Highly sensitive.

**Goal.** Dale intends to step back from daily operations in about four years. Preferred outcome is a sale to the service manager, or to an employee group, over a multi year payout. A sale to an outside buyer is the fallback.

**Rough valuation.** A broker estimate suggests 3 to 4 times adjusted annual earnings. Current adjusted earnings are about $410,000, so the range is roughly $1.2 million to $1.6 million before debt. The maintenance plan base of about 900 customers adds value.

**Steps**
1. Raise the service manager's pay and share profit to build his stake gradually.
2. Clean up the books for three straight years.
3. Move the customer list and plan renewals into a system that does not depend on any single person.
4. Talk to the attorney and the accountant about structure. Do not tell staff until a plan is in place.

**Risks.** Key customer relationships sit with Dale personally. Two senior technicians could leave if they hear about a sale from someone else. The commercial school bid, if won, would raise the value but also the workload.

**Who knows.** Only Dale, his spouse, and the attorney.
